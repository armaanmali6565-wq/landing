Risk Disclosure

Read this before sending any TRX. Your safety comes first.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Blockchain transfers are usually irreversible. Always verify the provider address, network (TRON Mainnet), and asset (TRX only) before you confirm.

1. Irreversible payments

Once TRX is sent on TRON Mainnet, it generally cannot be reversed. Sending to a wrong address, wrong network, or wrong asset can cause permanent loss of funds.

2. Send TRX only

  • The provider address accepts TRX for the sponsorship payment
  • Do not send USDT, USDC, or other tokens to that address
  • Tokens sent by mistake may not be recoverable

3. Address verification

Only use the provider address displayed on this ZeroTrx site (Pay page). Compare the full address character-by-character before sending. Phishing sites and lookalike addresses are a common risk in crypto.

4. Eligibility is not guaranteed

Paying 0.2 TRX does not automatically guarantee sponsorship for every transfer. Coverage depends on eligibility, supported tokens/contracts, resource availability, and network conditions. Typical network-fee examples (~18–20 TRX) are illustrative and can change.

4b. Refunds if sponsorship cannot be completed

If you paid the correct TRX amount to the official address and ZeroTrx cannot sponsor an eligible transfer, email support@sparq.energy with your transaction ID. See Terms for the refund and failure policy. Wrong-asset or wrong-address sends usually cannot be recovered.

5. Network and price variability

TRON resource costs, Energy requirements, and TRX market price can change. Your experience without ZeroTrx may differ from examples shown on the site.

6. No custody of your wallet

ZeroTrx does not hold your funds in a custodial account and does not need wallet connection. You remain responsible for securing your own wallet. Never share seed phrases or private keys with anyone.

7. No investment advice

ZeroTrx is a technical sponsorship service for eligible TRC-20 transfers. Nothing on this site is an offer to buy/sell securities or investment advice.

8. Independence

ZeroTrx is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the TRON Foundation unless explicitly stated.

9. If something looks wrong

  • Stop and re-check the official ZeroTrx URL and provider address
  • Do not send more funds until verified
  • Contact support@sparq.energy with your transaction ID if you need payment help
