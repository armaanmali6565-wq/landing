FAQ

Questions

Short answers about Energy, payment, and sponsorship.

Why do I need Energy?

TRC-20 transfers execute smart-contract operations that consume TRON network resources such as Energy.

Why does my USDT transfer cost TRX?

When sufficient Energy is unavailable, the network may require TRX to cover the corresponding resource cost for the smart-contract transfer.

How much do I pay?

0.2 TRX minimum.

What do I get?

One sponsored eligible TRC-20 transfer.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

No. You can send the required TRX manually to the displayed provider address.

What happens after I pay?

Payment is detected and confirmed, then the eligible sponsorship process can proceed.

How much does a normal transfer cost vs ZeroTrx?

Without sponsorship, a typical TRC-20 transfer can cost roughly 18–20 TRX in network fees. With ZeroTrx, you pay 0.2 TRX for an eligible sponsored transfer. Exact resource cost still varies by transaction, token contract, TRX price and network conditions.

Can I send USDT to the provider address?

No. Send TRX only to the provider address.
