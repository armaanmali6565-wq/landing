Terms of Use

Plain-language rules for using ZeroTrx safely.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

ZeroTrx does not ask for your seed phrase, private key, or wallet connection. You send TRX manually from your own wallet.

1. What ZeroTrx is

ZeroTrx is an independent TRON Energy sponsorship service. When you pay the stated minimum (currently 0.2 TRX) to the displayed provider address on TRON Mainnet, ZeroTrx may sponsor applicable network resources for one eligible TRC-20 transfer, subject to eligibility and resource availability.

2. What ZeroTrx is not

  • Not a bank, exchange, custodian, or investment product
  • Not affiliated with or endorsed by the TRON Foundation unless explicitly stated
  • Not a guarantee that every transfer will be sponsored
  • Not financial, tax, or legal advice

3. Payment rules (for your safety)

  • Send TRX only to the provider address shown on this site
  • Use TRON Mainnet only
  • Verify the full provider address before confirming in your wallet
  • Minimum payment is 0.2 TRX unless a different amount is clearly displayed
  • Do not send USDT, USDC, or any other token to the provider address
  • Wrong asset, wrong network, or wrong address may result in irreversible loss

4. Eligibility

Sponsorship applies only to eligible / supported TRC-20 transfers under current service configuration. ZeroTrx may decline or be unable to sponsor a transfer if it is unsupported, ineligible, or if resources are unavailable. Network-fee examples on the site (such as ~18–20 TRX) are illustrations only and can vary. Paying 0.2 TRX does not guarantee sponsorship.

5. Refunds and failed sponsorship

TRON transfers are usually irreversible. If you send the correct amount of TRX to the official provider address on TRON Mainnet and ZeroTrx cannot sponsor an eligible transfer (for example: ineligible transfer type, unsupported contract, or resource unavailability), contact support@sparq.energy with your transaction ID within 7 days. ZeroTrx will review the case and, where appropriate, either complete sponsorship when possible or arrange a TRX refund to the sending address. ZeroTrx does not refund wrong-asset, wrong-network, or wrong-address transfers, and does not recover funds sent to phishing lookalikes.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Confirm you control the wallet you use to send TRX
  • Double-check address, network, and asset before sending
  • Keep your own wallet security practices (never share keys or seed phrases)
  • Comply with laws that apply to you in your location

7. Service changes

ZeroTrx may update pricing, eligibility rules, supported tokens, or these terms. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised terms as published on this page.

8. Operator and contact

ZeroTrx is operated as an independent TRON Energy sponsorship service under the ZeroTrx brand. These terms are governed by the laws applicable to the operator’s place of business; a full registered entity name and governing jurisdiction will replace this sentence once company registration is complete. Until then, the operator remains reachable at support@sparq.energy · Support page.
